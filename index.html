Teacher plan · 25 minutes

Class: Grade 7 · Unit 2: Awesome animals · Lesson 3, part 1 (Grammar, Class Book)
Lesson aim: to learn modals for deduction.
Target language: may, might, could, can’t, must.
Materials: Unit 2 flashcards, grammar animation, traffic light cards, photocopiable 6, device or projector.

TimeStageWhat happensTeacher’s Book link
0–4Warm-up
Case 1: Zoom In		Review the animal body part vocabulary. Students see a zoomed picture and choose the word. Use the flashcards on the board too.“Starting the lesson”: place the Unit 2 flashcards on the board and quickly review the vocabulary.
4–9Presentation
Case 2: Traffic Lights		Write It can’t be a snake. It’s got fins. It must be a fish. Students drag modals to the green, amber or red light. Use traffic light cards in class.“Presentation”: explain that students will learn modals for deduction; ask which words tell us something is true or not true.
9–14Formative 1
Case 3: Complete the Rules		Students circle the correct options to complete the grammar rules, then check as a class.Activity 1: look at the grammar table, read and circle the correct options to complete the rules.
14–20Formative 2
Case 4: Mystery Clues		Students read clues and choose the right modal. Pairs say their own sentences. Use the Traffic light technique to check understanding.Practice with the grammar table and the Traffic light technique (page 17).
20–25Closing
Case 5: Spot the Modals		Students tap the modals in the Lesson 2 dialogue. Challenge: write own sentences. Exit ticket.Activity 2: read the dialogue in Lesson 2 and underline examples. “Finishing the lesson”: summative questions.

Differentiation

  • Support 🌱: two choices in Zoom In, colour hints on the traffic lights and in Mystery Clues, and pictures with the clues.
  • Standard 🌿: full activities.
  • Challenge 🌳: four choices in Zoom In, no hints, and students must write two of their own sentences with a modal in the last case.

Notes and keys

  • Rules: must = sure it is true · can’t = sure it is not true · may, might, could = not sure. Modal verbs are followed by the infinitive without to (e.g. must be).
  • Mystery Clues key: must, can’t, might, must, must, could, can’t.
  • The clues and rule sentences are written for this game. Compare them with the grammar table in your Class Book (page 40) and edit if needed.
  • Extra activity in the Teacher’s Book: groups imagine hearing strange noises at home and use modals to guess what is making them.
  • Deaf/hard of hearing students: use the traffic light cards and written clues; audio is optional.
